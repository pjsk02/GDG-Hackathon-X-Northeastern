## Potholes

Residents can report a pothole through the 311 app or by calling 311.
Crews aim to repair reported potholes within two business days.
Potholes larger than three feet across are routed to the roadway reconstruction team.

## Snow removal

During a declared snow emergency, parking is banned on posted snow emergency arteries.
Vehicles left on an artery during a snow emergency may be towed.
Sidewalks must be cleared within three hours after snowfall ends.

## Street lights

A broken street light can be reported through 311 with the pole number if visible.
Most street light outages are repaired within ten business days.
